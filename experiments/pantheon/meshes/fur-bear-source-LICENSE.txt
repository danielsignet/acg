The original GLB is preserved as fur-bear-source.glb. The static OBJ demonstration
is derived by fur_demo.py: baked default pose, Loop refinement of body/muzzle,
linear leg refinement preserving paw contacts, smooth normals and separate
grooming materials. The shell renderer and root texture are authored
in this project. The source page states no attribution is required; this notice
records provenance for reproducibility.
